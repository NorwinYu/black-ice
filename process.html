02 — The procedure

Built with the photoreal-game skill

A skill for turning a code-generated 3D scene into a short, playable game that reads as photographic: product first, then look; every judgement delegated to a gate; one lever per round.

Skill · photoreal-game

Not a template. A procedure.

The skill supplied the workflow this project actually ran on: a render-observable page contract, an art-direction brief, a kit-and-solids budget, and a sequence of executable gates. The game itself — the outpost, the snow system, the enemies, the loop, the shell — is original to this project. No reference image, model, texture or audio file was used.

Render-observableFixed cameras, a seeded clock, a page contract: __READY, __STATS, __PRODUCT, ?shot, ?cam, ?seed.
Product firstTitle, settings, pause, save, lost, won, touch and a complete objective chain existed before the final grading pass.
Gated, not opinionatedShaders, census, collision parity, light anchoring, playtest, product, mobile and look. A gate that lives only in a README is not run.
One lever per roundEvery iteration changed one thing and re-shot the fixed cameras. Renders were kept; regressions were reverted.
Declared look fallback. The brief forbade reference material, so no project reference profile was captured. The look gate runs in its declared fallback mode — tone (clipping, midtones) and emptiness (flat tiles) only — rather than borrowing another subject's energy profile.
03 — Iteration record

What changed, round by round

The first frame was nearly black. The snowcat once exploded into floating parts. A global replace once broke the start button. Each failure is kept here with the fix, because a build log without failures is a brochure.

Round 00 · shaders

Almost black

sampler2DShadow had no precision and FXAA was missing the noise function: world and sky programs failed. Fixed the precision and the include. The scene appeared.

Round 01 · composition

Make it readable

Moved the fence out of the camera and the fuel tank off the sightline; raised exposure; chose a camera with a foreground, a subject and a mast. Flat and pink, but finally readable.

Round 02 · transform stack

The snowcat exploded

Unbalanced at() calls accumulated transforms. Rewrote the vehicle with a balanced rbox() helper and reduced high-frequency paint and rust noise.

Round 03 · light

Move the key south-west

Every camera-facing face was in shadow. A south-west low sun lit the facades and the snowcat side, and the sky turned cold blue with an anti-solar horizon band.

Round 04 · world

Wind-packed snow

Height-field normals, sastrugi, boot prints, tyre ruts, a radome, cable reels and three jagged ridge layers. Flat tiles fell from 23% to 5%.

Round 05 · product

HUD, enemies, gun

First in-game frame. The rifle was huge, the reticle vanished and a crate blocked half the view. Scaled the viewmodel, thickened the reticle, moved the demo and the crate.

Round 06 · readability

See the enemy

Cyan shoulder/chest lamps, red visors and small position glows make hostiles legible at range. Softer tracks, deeper grading; the look gate's tone band was reached.

Round 07 · regressions

Two real bugs

The boss kill never scheduled the win (the hook set the flag early), and one global replace put a demo branch inside startGame(). Both fixed and re-verified. The late feature pass then exposed a third real bug: ribbon tracers at point-blank range became screen-filling triangles. They were replaced with additive sprite streaks, and the feature contact sheet was shot.

05 — Field notes

Controls, systems, honest limits

  • WASD move · SHIFT sprint · SPACE jump
  • Mouse look · LMB fire · RMB aim
  • R reload · E interact · Q / 1 / 2 switch tool
  • Esc pause · M mute
  • Touch left stick · right-drag look · on-screen actions

Systems in the build: 17 synthesized audio events with a master mute; best score and settings persisted locally; sensitivity, volume, FOV, invert-Y, quality and mute; quality presets for draw/shadow/snow budgets; mouse fallback via drag when pointer lock is unavailable; six shell screens and six demo/feature stills.

Known limits. Enemies are rigid-part rigs, not skinned meshes: readable at combat range, robotic up close. Audio is synthesized, not recorded. The narrow-screen HUD and the viewmodel can overlap the right-hand touch buttons at 390×844. There is no mid-run save, only best score and settings. Headless stills were rendered with SwiftShader; real-GPU frame rate was not measured in this build. The look gate is a declared fallback, not a photographic similarity score.

Privacy. No analytics, cookies, accounts, external fonts or third-party requests. The game writes only blackice.best and blackice.settings to the browser's local storage. The site is static.

Original work. Geometry, materials, sky, snow, UI, audio and game code were produced for this project. The photoreal-game skill supplied the procedure and gate design; its harness is credited but not redistributed here. No reference material or external asset was used.
